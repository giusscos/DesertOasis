## Description *(4000 chars max)*

Long ago, this land bloomed with life. Rivers carved green valleys, and a great oasis stood at the heart of it all. Then the desert came — relentless and slow — until only dust remained.

You are the last keeper of this place.

**Oasis Keeper** is a 3D desert exploration game about patience, kindness, and bringing life back to a dying land. Wander a vast procedurally generated desert, discover hidden springs and ancient landmarks, and carry water back to your camp — one bucket at a time.

**Explore a Living Desert**
Every save slot generates a unique world from a seed, so no two journeys are the same. Trek across sun-scorched dunes, stumble upon mirrored pools, shrine springs, and canyon wells — each one a secret buried in the sand.

**Restore Your Oasis**
Your camp's oasis starts as cracked earth. Bring water, and watch it grow — from a dry basin, to a camp pond, to a living oasis, to something flourishing and full of life. The desert remembers.

**Help the Wanderers**
You are not alone out here. Weary travellers pass through, merchants travel trade routes, and elders carry the memory of what this land once was. Give them water. Listen to their stories. Every act of kindness leaves a mark.

**Command the Animals**
Call camels and goats to follow your path with a magic stick. They are quiet companions for a long walk.

**Survive the Elements**
When the sandstorm rolls in, you wait it out. When the sun is merciless, you keep moving. The desert is not a puzzle to solve — it is a place to endure.

**Collect the Diary**
Scattered across the world are pages of an ancient keeper's diary — fragments of a time before the silence, when water ran free and neighbours gathered by the fire.

**Missions & Achievements**
Unlock missions from the elders and merchants you meet, or follow the quiet pull of the land itself. Earn achievements tracked through Game Center, and compete on global leaderboards for most oases restored, wanderers helped, and water deliveries made.

### 3. Purpose and Audience

Oasis Keeper is a 3D third-person desert exploration game. The player explores a procedurally generated desert, finds hidden water sources (oases, shrine springs, canyon wells), carries water back to their camp barrel, and watches a dead oasis slowly come back to life. Along the way they help wandering travellers, trade with merchants, survive sandstorms, and collect diary pages that reveal the land's history.

**Target audience:** casual exploration and simulation players who enjoy calm, unhurried gameplay with a sense of slow progress — similar in spirit to games like Stardew Valley or Alba: A Wildlife Adventure.

**Value:** offline-friendly single-player experience with clear progression (oasis growth stages), optional depth through missions and NPC dialogue, and Game Center leaderboards and achievements for players who want goals.

---

### 4. How to Access Main Features

No login credentials or sample files are required.

1. **Launch the app** (landscape orientation on iPhone).
2. **Intro story:** three slides play automatically; tap Continue or Skip.
3. **Lobby:** the camera animates through a 3D tent interior. Tap a save slot (up to 3 independent worlds). Name your character and begin.
4. **Desert:** move with the on-screen joystick. Explore dunes to find glowing oasis water sources. Approach one to collect water in your bucket.
5. **Camp:** return to camp and approach the barrel to pour water in. The oasis grows through four visible stages.
6. **NPCs:** wandering travellers, merchants, and elders approach camp. Walk up to them to trigger dialogue. Some offer missions; giving water to a wanderer completes a "help" interaction.
7. **Missions overlay:** tap the scroll icon to view active, completed, and failed missions.
8. **Animals:** equip the magic stick tool and approach a camel or goat — tap to call them to follow you.
9. **Sandstorm:** when weather triggers a sandstorm, stay in camp until it passes to earn the achievement.
10. **Game Center:** achievements unlock automatically in-game. The leaderboard entry point is in the main HUD. Core gameplay works fully without a Game Center account.

**Demo account:** none required.
**Game Center:** any Apple ID signed into Game Center on the review device. Sign-in is optional; the reviewer can ignore it entirely.
